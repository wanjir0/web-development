About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple place to capture, organize, and revisit the ideas you want to remember.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page and enter an idea in the New note field.
  2. Select Add Note to add your idea to the notes list.
  3. Review your notes whenever you need a quick reminder.

Features

  • Quick entry for ideas and reminders.
  • A clear list that keeps your notes easy to scan.
  • Keyboard shortcuts to help you work efficiently.
